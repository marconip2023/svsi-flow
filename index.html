Supplier Voluntary Self-Inspection, step by step

SVSI is moving from a Word-and-email process onto the ICW platform. One task covers one factory, and it runs through seven steps and four roles before that factory is allowed to inspect its own products. This page follows the designed screens in order — every card opens the real frame in Figma, so you can jump in wherever the discussion goes and find your way back.

Before the task exists

Buyer

SVSI applies to a factory, not a supplier. So the buyer starts from the supplier list, expands a supplier to see the factories underneath it, and decides which of those factories are in scope. Factories that will never join are marked Out of Scope; the rest sit at Not Rated until they earn a tier.

Application

Supplier appliesBuyer checks

The supplier starts the application themselves — this is the part that used to be the Word Application Form v4. They confirm the factory, run a self-assessment, pick the items the factory wants to self-inspect, and give payer details. The buyer then reviews the whole submission and either approves it, returns it for correction, or rejects it.

03

Lab Audit

Lab · UL / BV

The lab has its own view with its own lifecycle — Received → Audit Scheduled → Audit Completed → Report Uploaded → Agreement & Addendum Sign. They confirm the audit date, run the on-site factory assessment, then upload the report and the result. Findings are optional: Major NC, Minor NC and Observation / OFI entries are added only if there are any. Several reports may be uploaded, but there is one findings table and one result.

QT Review

Buyer · WBA QT

QT reads the lab's report and result and takes one of three routes: confirm and move on, request a revised report from the lab, or reject it. Only once the result is confirmed does the task carry an Audit Result, an Audit Report Completed Date, and a findings summary.

05

Ethical Check

Buyer sends

A separate annual product performance review, run alongside the factory audit rather than inside it. Phase 1 is an uploaded SVSI evaluation spreadsheet judged on 1506 for 52 weeks, VOC, star rating and the past year's inspection history. Phase 2 is meant to replace that upload with figures pulled from ICW for the buyer to verify.

Approve & Sign

Buyer generatesSupplier signsSVSI Team completes

Two documents come out of ICW. The Agreement is supplier-level and first-year only. The Addendum is factory-level and renewed every year — it is the list of exactly which products that factory may self-inspect. The supplier signs offline, the scans come back into ICW, and the SVSI team clicks Complete task. That click is what stamps the self-inspection validity dates and awards the tier.

Reference

The scheme ladder is a factory ladder

Products never carry a tier. They carry only New or Repeat.

TierHow a factory gets thereUL assessmentNew item uploadsRepeat item uploads
BRONZEFirst year in the programmeRequiredReport + Addendum—
SILVEROne good year at BronzeRequiredReport + AddendumReport + Addendum
GOLDOne good year at SilverNot requiredReport + AddendumAddendum only

So a Silver factory with a Repeat product still goes through the full lab flow. Repeat only lightens the document burden, and only at Gold does it drop the self-inspection report. An item graduates to Repeat by staying in the Addendum; drop it for a year and it comes back as New.

What the two documents actually ask for

Read from the Word originals, not the scans — the highlighted placeholders are real content controls.

Open questions for the team

Things the design cannot settle on its own.

  • Seasonal items are picked per WIC but written per category. Step 3 selects Seasonal items individually; the Addendum lists Seasonal only as a Product Category. Is that roll-up automatic, or does the buyer choose the categories again when generating?
  • A factory that changes every item every year still climbs to Gold. It would skip the audit while never holding a Repeat item long enough to earn the lighter upload rule. The deck does not cover this case.
  • Validity starts from the Addendum Effective Date, not the completion date. In the signed Greatstar example both fall on the same day. If they can differ, which one drives the validity window?
  • Lab wording is still UL-specific in places. "UL Audit", "UL — On-site Factory Assessment", "Factory previous UL assessment date". BV can now be selected, so these need neutral wording or a rule.
